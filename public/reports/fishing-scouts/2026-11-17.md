## Lake Taneycomo — The Trout Water
This cold tailwater, fed by deep releases from Table Rock, stays productive year-round thanks to consistent MDC stocking. Generation dictates tactics: low or minimum flows open wading, light-line drifting, or indicator fishing in seams and soft water; stronger pulses require added weight, drifting current breaks, and heavier line (2–4 lb test).

From Table Rock Dam down to Fall Creek is trophy water — **artificial lures and flies only; no soft plastics, scented, or natural baits permitted**. Rainbows 12–20 inches must be released. Save pink worms, PowerBait, or scented/scented baits for water **below Fall Creek**. A trout permit is required in many stretches. Recent reports note solid rainbow numbers with increasing brown trout in the upper lake on marabou jigs (black, sculpin, olive), zebra midges, scuds, small streamers, and eggs. Clarity is generally excellent. Always call 866-494-1993 for the latest SWPA generation schedule before you go and stay well outside the closed zone below the dam.
