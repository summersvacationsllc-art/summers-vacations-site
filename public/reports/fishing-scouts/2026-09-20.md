## Lake Taneycomo — The Trout Water
This tailwater trophy trout fishery stays productive year-round thanks to cold releases from Table Rock. Generation remains the primary tactic book: minimal or no flow overnight and early morning sets up classic light-line drifting and wading, while stronger current (often building through the afternoon and evening) calls for weighted rigs and drift fishing in seams and behind structure. Newly stocked rainbows are aggressive and provide fast action on a wide range of offerings.

Wade below the dam (outside the closed zone) during low periods with indicator rigs, micro jigs, or small flies. Drift boats and dock anglers score well when water is moving — stay in the current breaks and use heavier setups. Slot-limit rules apply in the upper sections (rainbows 12–20" protected; flies and artificials only in the trophy stretch from the dam to Fall Creek). Below the US 65 bridge, a trout permit is required to possess trout. Pink worms, zebra midges, scuds, small jigs, and egg patterns are all producing according to recent local reports. Water clarity is excellent — clean, natural presentations win. Black jigs after dark remain a reliable option. Call 866-494-1993 for the latest SWPA generation schedule before heading out.
