## Table Rock Lake
Warm water, clear conditions, and the ongoing drawdown have pushed bass, walleye, and crappie tighter to deeper main-lake structure. Recent patterns show fish holding on offshore rock piles, brush, roll-offs, and channel swings in the 15–25 ft range, though the bite can turn picky. Football jigs produce quality fish while drop-shots and Neds get numbers; covering water with good electronics separates successful trips. Low-light topwater remains effective around points and creek-arm backs. Walleye continue productive on gravel points with live bait or finesse presentations; schooling activity pops periodically on humps. Crappie relate to deeper timber. ## Lake Taneycomo — The Trout Water
The cold, clear tailwater fishery stays in reliable late-summer form per late-August reports from River Run Outfitters, John Sappington, and Lilleys' patterns. Low morning flows (typical overnight minimums) create excellent wading and dock fishing opportunities from below the dam downstream to the Landing and beyond. When generation ramps, shift to boat drifts targeting current seams, breaks, eddies, and deeper holding water. Pink Power Worms have been the consistent standout bait alongside micro jigs, midges (black, copperhead, rusty), scuds, eggs, and wooly buggers. Light line, careful depth control, and precise presentations are key in the gin-clear water — rainbows make up the vast majority of catches with some larger fish and occasional browns possible. Slot limits apply in the upper reaches. Dock fishing with floats or suspended jigs shines during minimal flow periods. **Always call 866-494-1993 for the live SWPA generation schedule before heading out** — it determines whether you wade light-line or drift heavier rigs in moving water. The trout water remains approachable, family-friendly, and productive when you match the flow.
