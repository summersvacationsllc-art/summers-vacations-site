## Lake Taneycomo — The Trout Water
This cold-water tailwater trophy trout fishery stays productive year-round thanks to cold releases from Table Rock and ongoing stocking. Generation remains the primary tactic book: low or minimal flows overnight and early morning create prime windows for wading, light-line drifting, and indicator fishing, while stronger current (often building afternoon through evening) calls for weighted rigs and drifting seams and structure. Newly stocked rainbows are aggressive; browns are beginning to show more as fall progresses.
