## Table Rock Lake
Summer heat, clear water, and the ongoing falling lake level have pushed bass, walleye, and crappie to deeper main-lake structure. Recent aggregates and angler patterns show fish present on offshore rock piles, brush, roll-offs, and channel swings in 15–25 ft, though the bite remains picky at times. Football jigs deliver bigger bites while drop-shots and Ned rigs produce numbers; covering water with electronics is the difference-maker. Topwater holds in the backs of creek arms and on rounded points during low light. Walleye continue productive on gravel points with live bait or drop-shot; schooling activity noted in several recent trips. Crappie hold around deeper timber. The pattern is a grind but productive for those who stay mobile and match the conditions. MDC rates black bass excellent for 2026 with plentiful fish — harvest spotted bass to help growth.

## Lake Taneycomo — The Trout Water
The cold, clear tailwater remains in solid late-summer form per John Sappington August overview and Lilleys' patterns. Low morning flows create excellent wading and dock opportunities from below the dam downstream. When generation kicks in, switch to boat drifts targeting seams, current breaks, and deeper channels. Pink Power Worms have been a standout bait alongside micro jigs, midges (black, copperhead, rusty), scuds, eggs, and PowerBait (chartreuse/pink). Use light line and control depth carefully — rainbows make up the bulk of the catch with some selective larger fish and occasional browns. Slot limits apply in the upper reaches. Dock fishing with floats or jigs shines during minimal flow. **Always call 866-494-1993 for the live SWPA generation schedule** before heading out — it dictates whether you're wading light or drifting heavier rigs. The trout fishery stays family-friendly with good catch rates on stocked fish when you match the flow.
