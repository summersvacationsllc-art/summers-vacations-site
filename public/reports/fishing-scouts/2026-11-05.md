## Lake Taneycomo — The Trout Water
This cold tailwater remains highly productive thanks to consistent MDC stocking. Generation is the primary tactic book: stronger flows favor weighted rigs, drifting seams, and targeting current breaks with 2–4 lb test. Lower windows allow lighter indicator rigs or wading.

From Table Rock Dam down to Fall Creek is trophy water — **artificial lures and flies only; no soft plastics or scented/natural baits**. Rainbows 12–20 inches must be released. Save pink worms, PowerBait, Gulp eggs, or other soft plastics/scented baits for water **below Fall Creek**. A trout permit is required in many sections. Recent reports highlight improving rainbow numbers, active browns in the upper lake, and success on marabou jigs, midges, scuds, worms, and eggs. Water clarity is generally excellent. Call 866-494-1993 for the latest SWPA schedule before heading out.
