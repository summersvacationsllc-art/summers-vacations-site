## Fishing Conditions & What's Biting
**Table Rock Lake (bass, walleye, crappie):**
- Summer heat pushes fish deeper or to structure: Target main-lake points, gravel banks, brush piles, channel swings, and creek arms in 15–35+ ft using electronics. Clear water favors finesse presentations.
- Recent reports (Captain Experiences/Fishing Booker August 2026): Walleye schooled on gravel points (strong numbers and quality reported); bass active early mornings on topwater/buzzbaits or deep with jigs, shaky heads, dropshot, and worms. Table Rock Fishing Intel notes dragging or deep techniques productive; MDC confirms excellent black bass fishing for 2026 with harvest encouraged on spotted bass. Early/late bites best before heat.
- Tips: Focus early topwater or deep structure during low-light; use electronics for suspended schools. Stable clear conditions great for family boating before heading to shows or the Branson Landing.

**Lake Taneycomo (trout focus):**
- Flow-dependent late-summer fishing holds up well per John Sappington August 2026 report. Low morning flows allow excellent wading, dock, and upper-reach fishing (Fall Creek to below dam productive). Heavier generation shifts to boat drifts in seams, deeper water, or below dam.
- What's biting: Good rainbow numbers (~90% of catch per MDC); selective larger fish and browns. Effective patterns include micro jigs (olive/gray/brown), midges (copperhead, black/red), scuds (gray/tan), hoppers (yellow/olive), wooly buggers, peach/pink eggs, small worms/nightcrawlers, and PowerBait on docks. Drifting eggs or using Provo rigs during generation; light line and careful presentation key on low water.
- Recent action (River Run Outfitters/Orvis as of mid-August, Fishing Booker): Nice fish on hoppers/dropper, midges, and scuds during low morning flows. Docks productive with jigs under floats when off; boaters do well drifting in current breaks. Slot limit in upper reaches (release 12–20" rainbows).
- Approach: Time trips to generation schedule (low early = wade/dock; moving water = weighted drifts/seams). Start early to beat heat and crowds. Excellent family/kids option on relaxed low-flow mornings.
