## Fishing Conditions & What's Biting

**Table Rock Lake (bass, walleye, crappie):**
- Summer heat and clear water push fish to deeper structure, main-lake points, gravel/brush piles, channel swings, and creek arms (target 15–35 ft). Electronics essential.
- Recent reports (Table Rock Fishing Intel 08/21 "Versatile Presentations" and 08/20 Extreme Outdoors forecast): Topwater bite (buzzbaits, especially black) still productive in shallower wood cover areas, especially early or during light generation. Offshore structure bite solid on main-lake and river points — rock piles, brush, roll-offs in 15–25 ft using football jigs, deep crankbaits, flutter spoons, and drop shot. Walleye remain strong on gravel points. MDC 2026 prospects rate black bass fishing as excellent with high numbers of largemouth and spotted bass (harvest encouraged on spots to improve growth). Early morning and low-light periods best before daytime heat.
- Insider tip: Cover water with buzzbait in wood during low-light; switch to finesse deep presentations during brighter hours. Families can combine a morning fishing trip with Branson Landing or a show.

**Lake Taneycomo (trout focus):**
- Late-summer pattern holding well per John Sappington August 2026 report. Low morning flows (common in current schedules) allow excellent wading, dock fishing, and upper-reach action from Fall Creek area to below the dam. Heavier generation shifts focus to boat drifts in current seams and deeper water.
- What's working (Orvis/River Run reports as of mid-August, FishingBooker August updates): Hopper/dropper combos (yellow/olive hoppers), midges (copperhead, black, red, rusty), gray/tan scuds, olive wooly buggers, micro jigs (olive/gray/brown), peach/pink eggs, small worms/nightcrawlers, and PowerBait on docks. Drifting eggs or using Provo rigs during generation. Light line (4–6 lb) and precise presentations key on low/clear water. Rainbows make up ~90% of catch (per MDC); selective on larger fish and browns. Slot limit applies in upper reaches (release rainbows 12–20").
- Approach: Align trips with SWPA schedule — low early morning for wading/docks with kids; moving water for weighted drifts. Start early to avoid heat and crowds. Strong family-friendly option with high catch rates on stocked trout.
