## Fishing Conditions & What's Biting
**Table Rock Lake (bass, walleye, crappie, etc.):**
- Summer heat, clear water, and falling level push fish to deeper main-lake points, gravel/brush piles, channel swings, creek arms, and offshore structure (target 15–35 ft). Electronics essential for locating shad schools and suspended fish.
- Recent reports (Table Rock Fishing Intel "Minimal Activity" 08/27, Captain Experiences, Cape Fair Marina patterns): Tougher bite on 8/27 with fish present but slow to commit. Versatile presentations still key — buzzbaits/topwater early or in wood cover during low light; switch to Ned rigs, drop-shot, football jigs, deep crankbaits, flutter spoons, or soft plastics (green pumpkin, goby, brown, natural shad) along bluff ledges, points, and channel banks in 10–25 ft. Walleye productive on gravel points with drop-shot or nightcrawlers. Crappie around deeper timber; bluegill shallow on docks. MDC 2026 rates black bass excellent (plentiful largemouth/spotted; harvest spotted bass to improve growth). Schooling activity noted in some recent trips.
- Insider tip: Focus upper river arms, points, and humps. Combine a morning trip with Branson family activities for the perfect multi-generational day from your Summers Vacations rental.

**Lake Taneycomo (primarily trout):**
- Late-summer pattern holding per John Sappington August 2026 and Orvis/River Run reports. Low morning flows create strong wading and dock opportunities from below the dam to Fall Creek and lower lake. Heavier generation shifts focus to boat drifts in seams, current breaks, and deeper channels.
- What's working: Pink Power Worms, nightcrawlers, micro jigs (olive/gray/brown), midges (black copperhead, rusty, red, tan/gray scud), eggs, hoppers/dropper, wooly buggers, PowerBait (chartreuse/pink/white), small spinners. Light line (2–6 lb test), precise drifts, and depth control key in the clear, cold water. Rainbows dominate (~90% of catch); selective larger fish and occasional browns/trophies. Slot limits apply in upper reaches (release rainbows 12–20"). Dock fishing productive during low water with floats/jigs/PowerBait. Recent catches include solid numbers on pink worms during low generation.
- Approach: Align strictly with SWPA generation schedule (low early for easy wading/docks/kids; moving water for weighted drifts in seams). Start early to beat the heat and humidity. Strong, family-friendly trout fishery with good catch rates on stocked fish.
