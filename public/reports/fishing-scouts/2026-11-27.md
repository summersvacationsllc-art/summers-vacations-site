## Table Rock Lake
The lake is down nearly 6 feet from full pool, exposing more shoreline and stumps in the coves and arms. Boat slowly in the James, Kings, and Long Creek arms. Surface water is cold now, pushing bass tighter to structure.

Look for them on long points, gravel flats dropping into deeper water, and offshore brush. Jigs, drop-shot rigs, and live bait are producing in 20-40 feet. Clarity is good, so keep presentations natural and slow. Mixed-bag opportunities for walleye and crappie remain around docks and deeper brush.

## Lake Taneycomo — The Trout Water
Cold releases from the bottom of Table Rock keep Taneycomo in the low 50s. Generation is the tactic book here: low morning flows allow wading or light-line drifting with indicators. When units come online, switch to heavier setups and target seams and current breaks from a boat or dock.

From Table Rock Dam down to Fall Creek it is artificial lures and flies only — no soft plastics and no scented or natural baits. Rainbows 12–20 inches must be released (slot limit). Save pink worms, PowerBait, or other soft plastics/scented baits for water below Fall Creek. A trout permit is required to keep fish. Always check the SWPA schedule or call 866-494-1993 before wading.
