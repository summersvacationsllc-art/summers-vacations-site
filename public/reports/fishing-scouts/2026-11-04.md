## Lake Taneycomo — The Trout Water
Cold bottom releases from Table Rock keep this tailwater in the mid-50s. Generation remains the complete tactic book: light early flows allow wading or light-line drifting with indicators. When turbines ramp (typically afternoon into evening), fish weighted rigs in the seams and current breaks.

From Table Rock Dam downstream to Fall Creek it is **artificial lures and flies only** — no soft plastics, no scented or natural baits. Rainbows 12–20 inches must be released. Save pink worms, PowerBait, or other scented options for water **below Fall Creek**. A trout permit is required in the upper sections. Micro jigs, midges, scuds, and small streamers in natural colors are working well. Call 866-494-1993 for the live schedule so rising water does not catch you off guard.
