## Lake Taneycomo — The Trout Water
This cold tailwater fishery stays productive year-round thanks to consistent MDC stocking and cold releases from the bottom of Table Rock Dam. Generation remains the primary tactic book: low or no generation allows lighter rigs, wading, or indicator fishing in the seams; stronger flows call for added weight, drifting the current breaks, and 2–4 lb test.

From Table Rock Dam down to Fall Creek is trophy water — **artificial lures and flies only; no soft plastics or scented/natural baits allowed**. Rainbows 12 to 20 inches must be released. Save pink worms, PowerBait, or other soft plastics/scented baits for water **below Fall Creek**. A trout permit is required in many areas. Recent patterns show solid rainbow numbers with increasing brown activity in the upper stretches. Marabou jigs, midges, scuds, small streamers, and eggs work well. Water clarity is generally excellent. Always call 866-494-1993 for the latest SWPA generation schedule before heading out and stay clear of the closed zone below the dam.
