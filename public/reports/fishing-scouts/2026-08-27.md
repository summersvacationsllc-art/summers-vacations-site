## Fishing Conditions & What's Biting

**Table Rock Lake (bass, walleye, crappie, etc.):**
- Summer heat and clear water push fish deeper to main-lake points, gravel/brush piles, channel swings, creek arms, and offshore structure (target 15–35 ft). Electronics highly recommended for locating shad schools and suspended fish.
- Recent reports (Table Rock Fishing Intel, Cape Fair Marina Aug 2026, Omnia/Extreme Outdoors): Versatile presentations working well. Buzzbait/topwater in wood cover and shallower areas during low-light or light generation (early morning/evening); switch to Ned rigs, drop-shot, football jigs, deep crankbaits, flutter spoons, or soft plastics (green pumpkin, brown, goby, natural shad) along bluff ledges, points, and channel banks in 10–25 ft. Walleye strong on gravel points with drop-shot/nightcrawlers. Crappie near timber in 12–15 ft; bluegill shallow (4–10 ft) on docks/timber with live bait or jigs. MDC 2026 rates black bass excellent with plentiful largemouth/spotted bass (harvest spots to improve growth). Night fishing productive on points with worms.
- Insider tip: Focus upper river, river flats, and creek mouths for bass; use electronics for shad. Combine morning fishing with Branson activities for families.

**Lake Taneycomo (primarily trout):**
- Late-summer pattern holding strong per John Sappington August 2026 and recent FishingBooker reports. Low morning flows create excellent wading/dock opportunities below the dam to Fall Creek and lower lake. Heavier generation shifts to boat drifts in seams, current breaks, and deeper water.
- What's working: Pink Power Worms, nightcrawlers, micro jigs (olive/gray/brown), midges (copperhead, black, red, rusty), scuds, eggs, hoppers/dropper, wooly buggers, PowerBait (chartreuse/pink/white), small spinners/spoons on docks. Light line (2–6 lb), precise drifts, and depth control key in clear water. Rainbows ~90% of catch (MDC); selective larger fish and browns. Slot limit in upper reaches (release rainbows 12–20"). Dock fishing solid during low water with floats/jigs/PowerBait.
- Approach: Align with SWPA schedule (low early for wading/docks/kids; moving water for weighted drifts). Start early to beat heat. Strong, family-friendly trout fishery with high catch rates on stocked fish.
