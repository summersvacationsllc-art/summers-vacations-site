## Lake Taneycomo — The Trout Water
This cold-water tailwater trophy trout fishery stays excellent with consistent stocking and cold releases from Table Rock. Generation remains the primary tactic book: minimal/low flows overnight and early morning enable light-line drifting, wading, and indicator fishing, while stronger current later calls for weighted rigs and drifting seams and structure. Newly stocked rainbows are aggressive; browns are starting to show with the fall transition. Recent reports note productive hopper fishing in warmer surface conditions and strong action on Mega Worms, San Juan Worms, and midges.

Wade below the dam (stay well outside the 760-ft closed zone) during low periods with micro jigs, pink worms under a float, or small flies (zebra midges productive). Drift boats and dock anglers excel when water is moving — hold in current breaks with weighted setups. Slot-limit rules apply in upper sections (rainbows 12–20" protected; flies and artificials only from dam to Fall Creek). Below the US 65 bridge, a trout permit is required to possess trout. Mega worms, San Juan worms, hoppers, scuds, zebra midges, pink/salmon jigs, and nightcrawlers on split-shot rigs are all producing per September reports. Water clarity is high — clean, natural presentations win. Black jigs after dark remain reliable. Call 866-494-1993 for the latest SWPA generation schedule before heading out.
