## Table Rock Lake
Summer heat, clear water, and the falling lake level have pushed bass and walleye to deeper main-lake structure. Recent reports (Table Rock Fishing Intel 8/27–28, FishingBooker trips through 8/29) show fish are present but the bite can be picky — offshore rock piles, brush, and roll-offs in 15–25 ft are the most reliable spots. Football jigs deliver bigger bites while drop-shots get more numbers; covering water with electronics is the difference-maker. Topwater holds in the backs of creek arms and on rounded points during low light. Walleye continue strong on gravel points with live bait or drop-shot; schooling activity noted in several recent trips. Crappie hold around deeper timber. The pattern is a grind but productive for those who stay mobile and match the conditions. MDC rates black bass excellent for 2026 with plentiful fish — harvest spotted bass to help growth.

## Lake Taneycomo — The Trout Water
The cold, clear tailwater remains in solid late-summer form. Low morning flows create excellent wading and dock opportunities from below the dam downstream. When generation kicks in, switch to boat drifts targeting seams, current breaks, and deeper channels. Pink Power Worms have been the standout bait in recent reports, with micro jigs, midges (black, copperhead, rusty), scuds, eggs, and PowerBait (chartreuse/pink) also producing well. Use light line and control depth carefully — rainbows make up the bulk of the catch with some selective larger fish and occasional browns. Slot limits apply in the upper reaches. Dock fishing with floats or jigs shines during minimal flow. **Always call 866-494-1993 for the live SWPA generation schedule** before heading out — it dictates whether you're wading light or drifting heavier rigs. The trout fishery stays family-friendly with good catch rates on stocked fish when you match the flow.
