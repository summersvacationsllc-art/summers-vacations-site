## Table Rock Lake
Warm surface water around 78°F, clear conditions overall, and the ongoing drawdown have pushed bass, walleye, and crappie tighter to deeper main-lake structure in the 15–25 ft range. Offshore rock piles, brush, roll-offs, and channel swings are holding fish, though the bite can turn picky in bright midday sun. Football jigs and drop-shots are producing quality bass while Ned rigs get numbers; covering water with good electronics and adjusting to generation-driven current is key. Low-light topwater remains effective around points and creek-arm backs. Walleye continue on gravel points with live bait or finesse; schooling activity shows on humps periodically. Crappie relate to deeper timber. As we edge closer to fall turnover, these offshore patterns often improve with small clarity or temperature shifts — it's a technical but productive grind that rewards preparation and mobility.

## Lake Taneycomo — The Trout Water
The cold, clear tailwater fishery remains in reliable late-summer form as September begins. Low morning flows (typical overnight minimums) create excellent wading and dock fishing opportunities from below the dam downstream. When generation ramps (often afternoons/evenings), shift to boat drifts targeting current seams, breaks, eddies, and deeper holding water. Pink Power Worms have been the consistent standout alongside micro jigs, midges, scuds, eggs, and wooly buggers. Light line, careful depth control, and precise presentations shine in the gin-clear water — rainbows make up the vast majority of catches with some larger fish and occasional browns possible. Slot limits apply in the trophy area upstream. Dock fishing with floats or suspended jigs works well during minimal flow. **Always call 866-494-1993 for the live SWPA generation schedule before heading out** — it determines whether you wade light-line or drift heavier rigs in moving water. The trout water stays approachable and family-friendly when you match the flow.
