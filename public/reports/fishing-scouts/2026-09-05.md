## Lake Taneycomo — The Trout Water
The cold, clear tailwater fishery stays in reliable late-summer form per John Sappington's September overview and Lilleys' Landing patterns. Low morning flows (typical overnight minimums) create excellent wading and dock fishing opportunities from below the dam downstream to the Landing and beyond. When generation ramps, shift to boat drifts targeting current seams, breaks, eddies, and deeper holding water. Pink Power Worms have been the consistent standout bait alongside micro jigs, midges (black, copperhead, rusty), scuds, eggs, and nightcrawlers. Recent trips boated 25–55+ rainbows using pink worms and split-shot rigs. Light line, careful depth control, and precise presentations are key in the gin-clear water — rainbows make up the vast majority of catches with some larger fish and occasional browns possible. Slot limits apply in the upper reaches. Dock fishing with floats or suspended jigs shines during minimal flow periods. Dry fly action with tiny midges improving in trophy areas along bluffs and eddies. **Always call 866-494-1993 for the live SWPA generation schedule before heading out** — it determines whether you wade light-line or drift heavier rigs in moving water. The trout water remains approachable, family-friendly, and productive when you match the flow.
