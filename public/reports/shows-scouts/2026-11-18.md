## Insider Tips
Book morning or matinee shows if you want to leave evenings free for dinner and lights on the strip. Many holiday productions sell out faster in November, so reserve tickets at least a day ahead, especially for Sight & Sound and popular tributes. Arrive 30–45 minutes early for parking and to soak up the festive lobby atmosphere—Branson traffic can pick up around show times.
