🎣 Table Rock Lake (Bass & General)

**Water Temp:** ~78-86°F (Kimberling City area ~78.7°F as of 7/25 evening; surface temps can reach mid-80s in river arms)
**Lake Level:** 916.5-916.6 ft (below power pool of 917 ft; stable in recent days per USACE)
**Clarity:** Clear to slightly stained in river arms
**Black Bass:** Strong early-morning topwater bite (Pop-R, walking baits, Whopper Plopper). Move to deeper brush piles, points, and shad schools with shaky heads, deep crankbaits, or spoons once the sun is high. Boat traffic and clear water require stealth.
**Catfish:** Evening/night bite along channels and near the dam using cut bait or stink bait.
**Crappie:** Suspended over deep timber and brush piles; use small jigs.
**Source:** USACE Little Rock District gage (latest readings ~7/23-7/25), tablerockfishingintel.com (7/25 conditions + 7/23 river report), captainexperiences.com and fishingbooker.com July reports.

🐟 Lake Taneycomo (Trout)

**Generation & Flow:** Variable—typically low/minimum flow early mornings with heavier afternoon/evening generation (call 866-494-1993 for today's schedule). Recent patterns show 20-12,000+ cfs swings.
**Water Temp:** Cold tailwater ~48-55°F from turbines; surface warms into the 70s downstream.
**Clarity:** Very clear—use light line (2-4 lb test or lighter).
**Current Flow:** Drift bait, flies, or bottom-bounce jigs when generation is running.
**Slack Water/Eddies:** Target Branson Landing area, Turkey Creek, and Scotty's Dock when flows are high. Fish rest in slower pockets.
**Trophy Area (Fall Creek to Cooper Creek):** Drift nightcrawlers, zebra midges, scuds, or small jigs. Rotate colors (pink worms, olive/gray midges, scuds) until you find the bite. Trophy browns are present.
