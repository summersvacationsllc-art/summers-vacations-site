Fishing

Bass on Table Rock hold on long points, gravel flats dropping into 20–40 ft, and offshore structure, hitting jigs, drop-shots, and live bait in the gin-clear conditions. Walleye and crappie continue producing around brush and points. On Taneycomo, stocked rainbows and increasing browns respond well to micro jigs, midges, scuds, and small streamers during low-flow windows.
