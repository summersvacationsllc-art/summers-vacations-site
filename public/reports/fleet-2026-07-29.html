🎣 Table Rock Lake (Bass & General)

**Water Temp:** ~78-86°F range (recent checks show ~78.3°F at Kimberling City on 7/28; main lake 82-86°F per late-July forecasts; surface warming in summer heat)
**Lake Level:** ~916.4-916.7 ft (near power pool of 917 ft; slowly fluctuating per latest USACE data accessed July 29)
**Clarity:** Mostly clear on main lake; river arms transitioning clear to slightly stained
**Black Bass:** Early morning topwater (Pop-R, walking baits, Whopper Plopper) remains productive. Transition to deeper brush piles, points, ledges, and shad schools (8-20+ ft) with drop-shot (live nightcrawlers best), Carolina rigs (6 worms in green pumpkin/brown/chartreuse/red), shaky heads, deep crankbaits, jigs (green pumpkin/black-blue), or soft plastic swim baits near shad. Electronics key for suspended fish; stealth important in clear water and boat traffic.
**Crappie/Bluegill/Walleye:** Crappie on small jigs/trailers (white/chartreuse/natural shad) near brush/timber (10-15 ft) or under shad schools late evening with small crankbaits. Bluegill active 4-10 ft near docks/timber with nightcrawlers/minnows or drop-shot. Walleye on extended points/ledges with drop-shot nightcrawlers near shad/Kentucky bass schools.
**Catfish/White Bass:** Catfish improving early/late/night on cut bait (small bluegill) or spoons at docks/flat points (3-15 ft). White bass in creeks under shad balls on crankbaits, Road Runners (chartreuse/white), or spinnerbaits.
**General:** Summer patterns dominant; good numbers reported in recent July captain feedback. Focus on stable weather and shad activity. Boat traffic heavy on weekends.

Call 866-494-1993 or check energy.gov/swpa for exact live schedule.
**Water Temp:** Cold tailwater ~48-55°F from turbines; surface warms downstream.
**Clarity:** Very clear — use light line (2-4 lb test) and finesse.
**Current Flow:** Drift bait, flies, bottom-bounce jigs, or streamers when generating.
**Slack Water/Eddies:** Target Branson Landing, Turkey Creek, Scotty's Dock, Fish House, and public docks/pier areas during high flows or no-generation. Fish seams and slower pockets.
**Trophy Area (Fall Creek to Cooper Creek):** Drift nightcrawlers, zebra midges, scuds, small/micro jigs. Effective colors: pink worms, olive/gray/brown midges/scuds, chartreuse/orange/white PowerBait. Small midges, scuds, and micro jigs in natural tones (olive/gray/brown) remain top producers in July. Good numbers and trophies reported; handle carefully.
**General:** Steady summer trout fishing; improved recently per reports. Nightcrawlers, eggs, and flies work well in variable flows.
