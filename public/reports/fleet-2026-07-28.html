🎣 Table Rock Lake (Bass & General)

**Water Temp:** ~79-80°F (79.8-79.9°F at Kimberling City as of recent checks per tablerockfishingintel.com)
**Lake Level:** 916.4-916.5 ft (near full pool / conservation pool ~917 ft; slowly falling per latest USACE data)
**Clarity:** Clear to mostly clear on main lake (some stain possible in river arms)
**Black Bass:** Strong early-morning topwater action (Pop-R, walking baits, Whopper Plopper). Transition to deeper brush piles, points, and shad schools with shaky heads, deep crankbaits, spoons, drop-shot, or jighead minnows as sun rises. Use electronics to locate suspended fish; clear water and boat traffic call for stealth.
**Crappie/Other:** Crappie suspended over deep timber/brush with small jigs. Bluegill active. Catfish on evening/night bite with cut or stink bait.
**General:** Good numbers and quality reported in recent captain feedback for July. Focus on stable weather windows.
**Source:** USACE Little Rock District (swl-wc.usace.army.mil and water.sec.usace.army.mil data for July 27-28, 2026), tablerockfishingintel.com (conditions checked ~07/27-28), johnsappington.com, fishingbooker.com, and aggregated July captain reports. Lake level cross-checked with USACE gage.

🐟 Lake Taneycomo (Trout)

**Generation & Flow:** Variable summer pattern — low/minimum flow early mornings (~20-100 cfs or less) transitioning to heavier afternoon/evening turbine generation (often thousands of cfs, up to 7,000-12,800+ cfs range in recent patterns). SWPA schedule for July 27 showed limited early loading on Table Rock Dam (TRD) with potential increases later; exact today's schedule subject to change. Call 866-494-1993 or check energy.gov/swpa for live updates.
**Water Temp:** Cold tailwater ~48-55°F from turbines; surface warms downstream into the 70s.
**Clarity:** Very clear — use light line (2-4 lb test) and finesse presentations.
**Current Flow:** Drift bait, flies, bottom-bounce jigs, or streamers when generating.
**Slack Water/Eddies:** Target Branson Landing area, Turkey Creek, Scotty's Dock, Fish House, and public docks/pier areas during high flows or no-generation periods. Fish seams, slower pockets.
**Trophy Area (Fall Creek to Cooper Creek):** Drift nightcrawlers, zebra midges, scuds, small/micro jigs. Effective colors: pink worms, olive/gray/brown midges/scuds, chartreuse/orange/white PowerBait. Good numbers and some trophies reported; handle carefully. Small midges, scuds, and micro jigs in natural tones remain top producers per July reports.
**Source:** johnsappington.com (July 2026 update), lilleyslanding.com (July 4, 2026 and June patterns), Orvis, ozarkanglers.com, SWPA schedules (energy.gov/swpa, phone 866-494-1993), and captain reports.
