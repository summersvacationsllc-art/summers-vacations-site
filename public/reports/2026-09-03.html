On the water

Table Rock Lake (bass, walleye, crappie, etc.): Summer heat, clear water, and falling level push fish to deeper main-lake points, gravel/brush piles, channel swings, creek arms, and offshore structure (target 15–35 ft). Electronics essential for locating shad…

Table Rock guideDawn topwater on points, then slide deep to brush and ledges. Confirm the lake level before you idle the coves.
Taneycomo guidePink worms on the morning low-flow window. When generation jumps, drift the slack seams. Call SWPA 866-494-1993 before you launch.
