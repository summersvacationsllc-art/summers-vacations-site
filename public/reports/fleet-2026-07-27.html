🎣 Table Rock Lake (Bass & General)

**Water Temp:** ~78-86°F (79.8°F at Kimberling City as of 07/27 afternoon per tablerockfishingintel.com)
**Lake Level:** 916.4-916.5 ft (slightly above full pool of 915 ft; stable/slowly falling per USACE and tablerock.uslakes.info)
**Clarity:** Clear to mostly clear (river arms and main lake clear)
**Black Bass:** Strong early-morning topwater bite (Pop-R, walking baits, Whopper Plopper). Move to deeper brush piles, points, and shad schools with shaky heads, deep crankbaits, spoons, or drop-shot once the sun is high. Boat traffic and clear water require stealth. Jighead minnow and deep patterns also productive.
**Crappie/Other:** Suspended over deep timber and brush piles; small jigs. Bluegill remains strong. Catfish on evening/night bite with cut or stink bait.
**General:** Excellent numbers and quality reported in recent July captain feedback; use electronics to locate suspended schools.
**Source:** USACE Little Rock District / swl-wc.usace.army.mil (latest ~07/27), tablerockfishingintel.com (current conditions 07/27), captainexperiences.com, fishingbooker.com, and Extreme Outdoors July reports. Lake level also cross-checked with tablerock.uslakes.info and tablerocklakelevel.com.

🐟 Lake Taneycomo (Trout)

**Generation & Flow:** Variable—low/minimum flow (~20 cfs) early mornings with heavier afternoon/evening turbine generation (often 7,000-12,800+ cfs). Call 866-494-1993 or check SWPA daily schedule for today's exact times (SWPA site available but primary source is phone/web links). Recent patterns show significant daily swings.
**Water Temp:** Cold tailwater ~48-55°F from turbines; surface warms into the 70s downstream.
**Clarity:** Very clear—use light line (2-4 lb test or lighter) and clean presentations.
**Current Flow:** Drift bait, flies, bottom-bounce jigs, or streamers when generation is running.
**Slack Water/Eddies:** Target Branson Landing area, Turkey Creek, Scotty's Dock/Fish House, and pier docks when flows are high or in no-generation windows. Fish hold in slower pockets and seams.
**Trophy Area (Fall Creek to Cooper Creek):** Drift nightcrawlers, zebra midges, scuds, small jigs, or micro jigs. Rotate colors (pink worms, olive/gray/brown midges/scuds, chartreuse/orange/white PowerBait). Excellent for numbers and trophies; handle carefully.
**Source:** johnsappington.com (July 2026 update), lilleyslanding.com (July 4, 2026 report + patterns), Orvis, ozarkanglers.com forums, and aggregated captain reports. Generation from SWPA (phone 866-494-1993 primary; web schedules confirmed available).
