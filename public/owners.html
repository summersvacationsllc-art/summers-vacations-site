About Summers Vacations

Founded by Brian Summers, Summers Vacations brings 26 years of paramedic, firefighter, and heavy rescue squad engineering expertise to Branson vacation rental management. That background is not a résumé line — it is how the work gets done: systematic problem-solving, calm under pressure, and precision when something breaks at an inconvenient hour. Same as on an emergency call: triage the issue, prioritize the next action, execute.

Unlike a traditional management company that takes over your listing (and your hard-earned reviews), Summers Vacations operates as a co-host. You remain the primary host on Airbnb and VRBO — keeping your ratings, Superhost status, and direct guest relationships — while we handle the operational heavy lifting: cleaning coordination, 24/7 guest communication (handled personally by Brian), pricing, and property care. You keep building equity in the listing, not just occupancy for a season.

Branson is not a generic STR market. Lakefront access, hillside properties, Highway 76 traffic, and show weekends all change what a guest needs. We know Table Rock Lake access, Indian Point, and what it takes for a family to leave a 5-star review after a busy weekend on the Strip.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is your management fee?

15% of collected revenue plus a $50 monthly technology fee. That covers pricing, guest communication, cleaning coordination, marketing support, and owner reporting. No Booking.com. No hidden channel fees from us.

How is this different from a management company?

A management company typically takes over the listing. With co-hosting, you remain the primary host on Airbnb and VRBO. You keep the ratings, Superhost status, and repeat guests. We run operations. Brian still handles guest relations himself.

Do you handle all guest communication?

Yes. Brian Summers personally handles inquiries, check-in, checkout, and emergencies. You get pulled in only for owner-level decisions, or if you ask for updates.

What areas of Branson do you cover?

Branson West, Indian Point, Highway 76, Branson Hills, Table Rock Lake, Hollister, Rockaway Beach, and nearby communities. If you are not sure, just ask.
